# OFFICIAL FIRST INFORMATION REPORT (FIR) STANDARD STRUCTURE
(Under Section 154 Cr.P.C. / Section 173 Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita - BNSS)

1. District, Police Station, Year, FIR No., Date & Time of FIR Registration.
2. Acts & Sections:
   - Specific statutory penal sections (e.g., IPC / BNS Sections).
3. Occurrence of Offence:
   - Day, Date from, Date to, Time from, Time to.
   - Information received at Police Station (Date, Time).
   - General Diary Reference Entry No. and Time.
4. Type of Information: Written / Oral / Spoken Statement / Audio Recording.
5. Place of Occurrence:
   - Direction and distance from Police Station.
   - Beat No. / Village / Street / Locality / Landmark.
   - City / Taluk / District / State.
6. Complainant / Informant Details:
   - Name of Complainant.
   - Father's / Husband's / Guardian's Name.
   - Date of Birth / Age.
   - Nationality.
   - Occupation.
   - Address (Present & Permanent) with contact number.
7. Details of Known / Suspected / Unknown Accused with Full Particulars:
   - Name(s), Alias(es), Physical description, Known whereabouts, Relationship to victim if any.
8. Reasons for Delay in reporting by the Complainant / Informant (if any).
9. Particulars of Properties Stolen / Involved (with description and approximate valuation).
10. Total Value of Properties Stolen / Involved.
11. Inquest Report / U.D. Case No. (if any).
12. First Information Contents (Brief Statement / Narrative of the Complainant in chronological order).
13. Action Taken / Investigation Officer Assigned:
    - Name, Rank, Badge No., Police Station.
    - Verified and Approved by Station House Officer (SHO) / Inspecting Officer.
